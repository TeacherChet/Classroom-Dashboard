How do I connect a Google Sheet?
  1. Make a Sheet with two tabs named exactly Roles and Events.
  2. Roles tab headers: RoleLabel, StudentName — one row per student, row order = rotation order.
  3. Events tab headers: Title, Date, Note — dates as YYYY-MM-DD, column formatted as Plain Text.
  4. Share → General access → "Anyone with the link" → Viewer. Keep the link private since it shows student names.
  5. Paste the Sheet's URL above and click Connect.

Everything here saves automatically in this browser. Use "Backup data" occasionally to keep a copy you can restore from on another computer.
